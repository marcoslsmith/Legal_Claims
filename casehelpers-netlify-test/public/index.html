Common questions

Is this an SSDI application?

No. This is a request for a callback from CaseHelpers. The Social Security Administration decides SSDI eligibility.

Who would represent me?

After your callback, CaseHelpers may be able to connect you with an independent disability attorney or qualified representative. If they take your case and you choose to work with them, they can help prepare your application or appeal, gather records, and represent you before Social Security. You decide whether to hire them.

Is the screening and callback really free?

CaseHelpers does not charge you to submit this form, speak with our team, or be connected with an independent representative. Some representatives charge a fee only if your claim succeeds. Social Security generally must authorize fees for work on your claim before the agency, and some expenses, such as obtaining medical records, may be separate. Ask the representative to explain any fees and expenses before you sign an agreement. See Social Security’s guide to representative fee agreements.

What happens to my answers?

CaseHelpers uses them to respond to your request and discuss options for getting help. We ask for separate permission before sharing them with an independent disability representative.
