Privacy notice

Who we are

Casehelpers AI operates CaseHelpers, an independent marketing service that helps people seeking Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) assistance connect with independent attorneys or disability representatives.

What we collect

When you submit the form, we collect your name, phone number, screening answers, and a record of your permission to be called. Screening answers include age range, work history, benefits and representation status, and limited information about whether a condition affects your ability to work and whether you receive treatment. The form does not ask for diagnoses, medical records, or a Social Security number.

We may record campaign tags or ad click identifiers in the link you used to reach us. Our hosting and database providers may process technical information to operate and protect the site.

How we use and share it

We use your information to respond to your request, prepare for the callback, discuss options for getting help, prevent misuse, document your consent, and measure how people find the site. Our staff use a private queue to review requests. Netlify hosts the site and processes submissions. Supabase stores submissions for our team.

If we can connect you with an independent attorney or representative, we’ll tell you who they are and what information we propose to share, and ask for your permission first. We may also disclose information when legally required. The form does not send your contact details or screening answers to ad platforms.

Retention and choices

We retain information as needed to respond to your request, maintain consent records, protect the service, and meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to withdraw permission for future calls about your request or ask to access, correct, or delete information. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a privacy request.
